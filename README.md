# 🎬 IMDb Movie Sales Analysis using Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze IMDb movie data and uncover insights into movie releases, revenue performance, ratings, genres, runtime, votes, and top-performing movies.

The dashboard helps transform raw movie data into meaningful business insights using data visualization, DAX measures, and interactive filtering.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze movie releases across different years
- Understand revenue trends and performance
- Compare movie ratings and audience votes
- Analyze genre-wise movie distribution
- Identify top-performing movies and directors
- Explore relationships between rating, revenue, and runtime

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Power BI
- DAX
- Data Cleaning & Transformation
- Power Query
- Data Visualization
- Interactive Slicers
- KPI Cards
- Charts & Advanced Analytics

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Movie Overview
- Total Movies
- Total Revenue
- Average Metascore
- Average IMDb Rating
- Average Runtime
- Year-wise movie releases and revenue trends

### 2. Genre & Rating Analysis
- Movie distribution by genre
- Average rating by genre
- Average Metascore by genre
- Genre-wise revenue and votes analysis

### 3. Top Performers & Advanced Analysis
- Top 10 movies by revenue
- Top 10 directors by total revenue
- Top 10 movies by IMDb rating
- Top 10 movies by votes
- Revenue vs IMDb rating
- Runtime vs revenue analysis

### 4. Correlation & Advanced Metrics
- Revenue vs rating by genre
- Revenue by year and genre
- Revenue pattern by average rating
- Average rating by runtime

## 🔍 Key Insights
- 2016 recorded the highest number of movie releases with **297 movies**.
- 2016 generated the highest total revenue of approximately **$11.21K million**.
- The dashboard identifies top-performing movies based on revenue, ratings, and votes.
- Genre-wise analysis helps understand audience preferences and revenue contribution.
- Advanced visuals explore relationships between movie ratings, runtime, and revenue.

## 💡 Business Outcome
This dashboard enables stakeholders to evaluate movie performance, identify successful genres and directors, understand audience engagement, and support data-driven decisions in the movie and entertainment industry.
